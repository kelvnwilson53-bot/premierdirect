Your charges

Your call-out charges

Enter the charges your receptionist may quote. Add the days, times and any exceptions that apply. You can change these details whenever your prices change.

Your receptionist will only quote prices you enter here. If a price is blank or a job needs an assessment, she will explain that you need to confirm the cost. She will never invent a charge or promise a final repair price.

Payment setup

Payment links and payment confirmations are two separate steps. Read both so you know what your receptionist needs.

1. Connect your payment provider

Connect your payment provider to allow your AI receptionist to send customers a secure payment link.

Your payment provider (for example, Stripe or Square) handles the card details and payment. You must connect your own provider and authorize TriDashAI before your receptionist can send payment links. This connection is not active yet.

2. Payment confirmation

Allow your AI receptionist to confirm to customers when their payment has been successfully received.

The receptionist must receive confirmation from the payment provider that the payment succeeded. Sending a payment link does not mean the customer has paid. This confirmation feature depends on the payment-provider integration.
