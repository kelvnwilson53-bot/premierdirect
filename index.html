Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and using PremierDirect AI.

How does the setup work for my business?

Setup takes less than 5 minutes. You simply activate conditional call forwarding on your existing mobile or landline phone so unanswered calls automatically forward to your PremierDirect AI text engine.

Do I need to change my business phone number?

No! You keep your current business phone number. PremierDirect AI works seamlessly in the background whenever you are unable to answer a call.

How does Google Review Automation connect to Google?

You simply paste your Google Business Profile review link into your PremierDirect AI dashboard. After a appointment or service, tap "Send Review Request" to send a personalized SMS with your link straight to the customer.

Can I customize the automatic text messages?

Yes. You can edit the exact message template to fit your business voice, include your booking link, or ask specific questions (e.g., "Hi, thanks for calling Studio 8! How can we help you today?").

Are there long-term contracts or hidden fees?

None at all. PremierDirect AI is a simple $39/month flat rate. You can cancel at any time directly from your account dashboard with zero cancellation fees.
